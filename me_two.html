Gardner Thompson

A few years ago I went to Washington D.C. to tour the national mall since I hadn't been there since my middle school's 8th grade trip. The trip was inspired by my recent acceptance into law school. I stayed at a Marriott hotel in the city center on the 9th floor and had a great view of the building across the street. Something that stood out to me was that despite the city's size, it did not feel busy or crowded, and even finding a parking spot was easy.

Favorite Restaurant

Chick-fil-A

This feels a little unimaginative, but I really can't think of another restaurant that I love that hasn't closed down recently. I love Chick-fil-A because it's consistent and you always know what you're getting. If something isn’t right, they will make it right, and they also have great customer service.
