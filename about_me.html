A few years ago I went to Washington D.C. to tour the national mall since I hadn't been there since my middle school's 8th grade trip. The trip was inspired by my recent acceptance into law school. I stayed at a mariott hotel in the city center on the 9th floor and I had a great view of the building across the street from me. Something that stood out to me about visiting D.C. was despite the city's size, it did not feel busy or crowded, and even finding a parking spot was easy.
